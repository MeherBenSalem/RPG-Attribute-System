# RPG Attribute System 5.1.3

## Fixes

### Stats key missing from its own Controls section (Minecraft 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3)

On the 26.x builds, the "Open Stats Menu" key (default K) was listed under vanilla's **Miscellaneous** category
instead of RAS's own **Memory Of The Past** section in Options > Controls > Key Binds. Players looking for the RAS
section to rebind the key, for example when another mod also uses K, could not find it. The key now has its own
**Memory Of The Past** section again on Fabric and NeoForge, as on 1.20.1 and 1.21.1.

A key you had already rebound keeps your choice.

No config or save changes.
